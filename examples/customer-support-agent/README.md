This agent answers customer tickets, fetches knowledge-base pages, and sends email replies. Without policy, a prompt-injected ticket could make it auto-issue a refund, dump customer data into an outbound reply, or blast an internal all-staff list. This warranty.md holds every email for human approval and restricts recipients to customer and support domains (fail-closed, including on a missing recipient), denies listed internal broadcast addresses, denies refund execution strings so refunds reach a human only through the approval hold, denies listed PII marker and customer-table strings (case-sensitive substrings, defense in depth), requires a declared job type, and keeps execution limits low so one ticket cannot fan out.
